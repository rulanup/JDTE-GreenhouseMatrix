# Changelog

### English

#### 0.1.0-alpha1 (Current)

- **New**: The Creative Greenhouse (`jdte_matrix:creative_greenhouse`) moved from JDTE into this addon. It supplies creative-mode resources from reusable template slots: inserting a valid seed or plant template builds an inexhaustible output catalog without FE, Time Fluid, or other consumables. Four template inputs, 16-64 paged extraction-only outputs, eight standard upgrade slots (Capacity, Overclock, Fortune, Seed Conversion, Essence Conversion, AE Output), pipe/hopper/AE compatibility through the shared `jdte:greenhouse` crop resolution rules, and the JDTE upgrade/Auto I/O/AE2 integration.
